PARADIS & FACTIONS.

Learn how the major Beyond SMP factions are organised and where each side fits into the world.

🏛️ Paradis

Paradis is the main Attack on Titan-inspired faction of Beyond SMP. Its main base is Sina.

Players on the Paradis side can progress through the military structure and choose a military branch.

🎖️ Paradis Military

  • Scout Regiment
  • Military Police
  • Garrison Regiment

These are the three main Paradis military branches.

🌊 Bender Nations

The Bender side is divided into four nations:

  • Earth Kingdom
  • Fire Nation
  • Air Nomads
  • Water Tribe

⚔️ Titan Shifters

Titan Shifters are another major side of Beyond SMP. Players can choose from the available Titan types, subject to the server's Titan rules and availability.

🤝 Factions

Beyond SMP is built around multiple factions inspired by Attack on Titan and Avatar: The Last Airbender.

Beyond SMP motto: Trust is earned. Alliances are temporary.
