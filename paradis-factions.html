PARADIS & FACTIONS.

Beyond SMP has three major factions: Paradis, Titan Shifters and Benders. The server is inspired by Attack on Titan and Avatar: The Last Airbender.

Paradis

Paradis is based on the island from Attack on Titan. Players can live as citizens or enter the military system.

  • Citizen: civilian life, businesses, homes, food, money and the wider economy.
  • Cadet: military training and progression toward a branch.
  • Scouts: explore beyond the walls, take expeditions, discover locations, fight Titans and gather resources.
  • Garrison: protects the walls and civilians during Titan breaches and patrols/protects the walls.
  • Military Police: protects the Commander and important people/locations and can be involved in arrests after court proceedings.

Paradis Military

The planned military roles include Commander, Section Commander, Scout Commander, Garrison Commander, MP Commander and Walls Commander. Exact hierarchy between the branch-specific roles is still being finalised.

The Commander is the top Paradis leadership role, rather than the owner of the entire Beyond SMP server. Server staff have authority over the Commander when server moderation is involved.

Cadets

Cadets train and are tested before choosing a military path. Only the top 10 Cadets are planned to be eligible to choose the Military Police, following the anime-inspired concept. Other Cadets can choose another branch. A Cadet capacity of around 50 at a time is currently being considered, while Citizens are intended to be unlimited; both are subject to change.

Paradis Economy

Citizens can get a business permit, start or join a business, earn money, buy food and homes, and take part in the wider economy. Players may also be able to create their own towns, but planned town creation has limits and requires notifying the relevant people at the Society Building.

Society Building

The Society Building is planned to have four sections: Bank, Permits, Estate Agents and one section still to be decided.

Titan Shifters

Titan Shifters are their own faction. There are planned to be 9 Shifter Titans, with the Attack Titan handled by a separate system. The current Danny's AOT setup is expected to provide the available Titans except Cart and Founder, which are required for the server to launch.

Players enter the Warrior Candidate system at the spawn selection area. Candidates compete through challenges, with a leaderboard updated after challenges. When a Shifter Titan becomes available, the highest eligible Candidate gets first choice. A grouped selection period of roughly four weeks is being considered.

Benders

Benders are their own faction. The four elements are Fire, Water, Earth and Air, corresponding to the Fire Nation, Water Tribe, Earth Kingdom and Air Nomads. The nations are sub-factions within Benders, similar to the military branches within Paradis. Nation bases and leadership are planned, with some details still being finalised.
