PARADIS & FACTIONS.

Beyond SMP's Three Factions

Beyond SMP is inspired by Attack on Titan and Avatar: The Last Airbender, with three main factions:

  1. Paradis
  2. Titan Shifters
  3. Benders

Paradis

Paradis is based on the island from Attack on Titan. Its main experience is built around living as a citizen or joining the military.

When choosing Paradis, players choose Cadet or Citizen at the spawn selection area.

  • Citizens: can live life on Paradis, earn money, buy houses and take part in the wider civilian world.
  • Cadets: train to eventually choose a military branch.
  • Scouts: explore beyond the walls.
  • Garrison: protects the walls.
  • Military Police: guards Sina.

The faction base will be the main place for faction activities.

Paradis Ranks

The currently planned hierarchy is:

Commander → Section Commander → Cadet → MP / Garrison

Additional ranks have not been finalised yet.

Titan Shifters

Titan Shifters are their own faction. The current system allows 8 Shifter slots, with the Attack Titan handled separately.

Players become Warrior Candidates and compete to reach the top of the class. When a Shifter Titan becomes available, the top eligible Warrior Candidate can receive it.

Benders

Benders are their own faction and use the four bending types from Elementals:

  • Air
  • Water
  • Earth
  • Fire

Players choose their faction through the spawn selection area. More detailed Bender nation information is still being finalised.
