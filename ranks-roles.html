RANKS & ROLES.

Roles exist across server staff, Paradis military and Titan Shifter systems. Some roles are still being finalised.

Server Staff

  • Owner — top server authority.
  • Admin — higher-level staff with broad server responsibilities.
  • Moderator — player/community moderation, reports, assistance and some recorded tests.
  • Additional staff roles are still TBD.

Staff authority is above faction leadership. For example, the Paradis Commander leads Paradis but does not outrank Beyond SMP staff.

Paradis Military

Planned roles include:

  • Commander — top Paradis leadership role.
  • Section Commander
  • Scout Commander
  • Garrison Commander
  • MP Commander
  • Walls Commander
  • Cadet
  • Military Police
  • Garrison
  • Additional military roles are TBD.

Titan Shifters

  • Shifter
  • Honourable Marlian — exact ordering may change.
  • Warrior Candidate

Promotions & Removal

Promotion systems are still being designed. Players can potentially be demoted or removed from faction roles, including through disciplinary processes and court outcomes.
