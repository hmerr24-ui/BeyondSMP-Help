CLAIMS & PROPERTIES.

Claims

Beyond SMP uses Open Claims & Parties for land protection and shared claims.

To claim a chunk, use the configured claim key (currently the ' key) and select Claim, or use Xaero's map and right-click the desired area to claim the chunk(s).

Claim Limits & Protection

The current planned limit is around 64 chunks, although this may change.

Players cannot place or destroy blocks on another player's claim unless they have been given the appropriate access.

Spawn and other protected areas are server claims.

Claim expiry/settings and the final claim costs are still being decided.

Unclaiming

You can unclaim using the claim system's command or by using the ' key and selecting the unclaim option.

Shared Claims

Open Claims & Parties can be used for shared claims. Parties are used for team/shared-claim purposes, while SimpleTeams handles the actual team system.
