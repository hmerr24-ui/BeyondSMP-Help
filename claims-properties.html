CLAIMS & PROPERTIES.

Protect your builds, manage land and create your own property.

🛡️ Claiming Land

Beyond SMP uses Open Claims & Parties. Open the claim interface with the ' key, then claim a chunk. You can also use Xaero's Map: right-click the area and select the claim option.

🗺️ Multiple Chunks

Players can claim multiple chunks. The exact maximum is still being finalised.

🤝 Shared Claims

Open Claims & Parties also supports shared/party claims. Detailed permissions will be documented once finalised.

🏛️ Server Claims

Spawn and other protected areas can use server claims, controlled by the server.
