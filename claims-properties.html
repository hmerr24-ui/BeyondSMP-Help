CLAIMS & PROPERTIES.

Claims protect player builds and property from unwanted building, breaking and container access.

Claiming Land

The current planned limit is 64 chunks per player, although this may change and may potentially scale with rank. Players can claim through the claim-management key or through Xaero's map.

  • Press ' to open claim management and use the claim option.
  • In Xaero's map, right-click the desired area and choose the claim option.
  • Individual chunks can be unclaimed, and whole claims can be removed.

Permissions

Claim owners can grant and remove permissions. Players without permission cannot place or destroy blocks or open containers on another player's claim.

Shared claims can be managed with Party (OCAP). The exact ownership/permission details are still being finalised.

Server Claims

Spawn, faction bases and other protected locations such as the Bank can be server claims. Players cannot normally build, destroy or open containers there unless the server explicitly permits it.

Shifter Titans cannot transform inside protected server claims under the planned rules. Bending and PvP restrictions for claims are still being finalised.

Claim Costs & Limits

Extra chunks may potentially be purchasable with in-game money. Creation, expansion and maintenance costs are not finalised. Claim expiry is also still being investigated.
