GETTING STARTED.

Everything you need to know before joining Beyond SMP.

🎮 What You Need

  • Minecraft Java Edition
  • Minecraft 1.21.1
  • The Beyond SMP modpack
  • A Discord account
  • Your Minecraft and Discord accounts linked through Simple Discord Link

🌐 Server & Discord

Server: play.beyond-smp.com

⚠️ The server is not joinable yet. This address will be used when Beyond SMP launches.

Discord: discord.beyond-smp.com

📦 Modpacks

Essential — essential mods only; recommended for lower-tier PCs.

Enhanced — essential mods plus extra quality-of-life features and shaders; recommended for mid-to-high-end PCs.

The packs are distributed through Modrinth. The link is available through Discord, with mod.beyond-smp.com planned as a redirect. The Modrinth App is recommended.

🚀 Joining

When the server is live, go to the faction selection area at spawn and choose your faction. You cannot change faction during a season. You will then be directed toward your faction's starting area.

📝 Requirements

There is currently no additional age requirement or special requirement beyond getting the modpack, joining Discord, and linking your Minecraft and Discord accounts.
