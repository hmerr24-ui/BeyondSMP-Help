WORLD & LOCATIONS.

Beyond SMP's world combines the Attack on Titan and Avatar-inspired settings with an evolving Minecraft world.

Paradis

Paradis is planned as a separate dimension based on the island from Attack on Titan. The walls surround the main Paradis territory, with cities and settlements including Sina and Shiganshina.

Factions & Territories

  • Paradis has its own territory and faction bases.
  • Bender nations have their own territories.
  • Titan Shifters have their own area/base.

Spawn & Selection

Normal spawn is always available. The spawn selection area is separate and is only accessible at the beginning of each season, when players choose their faction and path.

Player-Built Towns

Players may be able to create their own towns, subject to limits. Planned town creation requires notifying the relevant people at the Society Building before establishing the town.

Protected Locations

Some areas are server claims. Spawn, faction bases and locations such as the Bank are examples of protected areas.

Society Building

The Society Building is a planned central civilian facility containing the Bank, Permits office, Estate Agents and a fourth section that is still TBD.
