💬 Need Help?

For support, reports, questions, or problems that need staff attention, contact the Beyond SMP staff team through Discord.

🆘 The easiest way: Join the Beyond SMP Discord server and open a ticket. Staff can then review your issue and help you directly.

📋 What to Include

  • Explain what happened.
  • Include relevant details such as the server feature or system involved.
  • If appropriate, include screenshots or other useful evidence.
  • Be patient while staff review your ticket.
