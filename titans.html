THE TITANS.

Shifter Titans

Beyond SMP uses Shifter Titan to distinguish Titan Shifters from other Titan-related terminology.

The current plan has 8 Shifter Titan slots, with the Attack Titan handled by a separate system. The available Shifter Titans are based on the Titans currently supported by Danny's Attack on Titan mod; Cart and Founding are not currently confirmed as available.

Becoming a Shifter

Players become Warrior Candidates. The top of the Warrior Candidate class can receive an available Shifter Titan.

Shifter selection is not intended to be swapped between friends; the class ranking is used for eligibility.

Attack Titan Events

Attack Titan events are planned to occur weekly or every two weeks. Winning the Attack Titan event can determine who holds the Attack Titan.

Shifter challenges/tests are recorded by a moderator and sent to the Owner for approval or denial.

Abilities & Transformation

Press B (or your configured key) to transform, then use B + crouch for the relevant transformation mechanic.

Abilities use the number keys. The exact ability list and energy values are still being finalised.

Energy

Shifting uses energy. When the available energy is depleted, you cannot shift again until it regenerates over time.

Protected Areas

Some server-claimed areas are protected from Shifter Titan transformation.
