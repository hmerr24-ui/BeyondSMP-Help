🌊 The Four Nations

The Bender side of Beyond SMP is divided into four nations:

  • 🌍 Earth Kingdom
  • 🔥 Fire Nation
  • 🌪️ Air Nomads
  • 💧 Water Tribe

🌀 Bending

Bending is the Avatar-inspired power system used by the Bender side of Beyond SMP.

Players can use the bending type associated with their character and faction.
