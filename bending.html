BENDING.

Learn about the four Bender nations and the Bending system.

🌊 The Bender Faction

Benders are one of the three major factions, inspired by Avatar: The Last Airbender. They are separate from Paradis and Titan Shifters.

🌀 The Four Nations

  • 🌍 Earth Kingdom
  • 🔥 Fire Nation
  • 🌪️ Air Nomads
  • 💧 Water Tribe

Players choose their nation at the selection area at spawn.

⚡ Elemental Bending

Beyond SMP uses Elementals for Bending.

⚔️ Combat

Benders can use weapons as well as their Bending abilities.

🚧 More Mechanics Coming

Detailed abilities, progression, commands and other mechanics will be documented once the exact setup is finalised.
