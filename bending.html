BENDING.

Bending is one of Beyond SMP's three major factions, powered through the Elementals system.

The Four Elements

  • 🔥 Fire — Fire Nation
  • 💧 Water — Water Tribe
  • 🌍 Earth — Earth Kingdom
  • 🌪️ Air — Air Nomads

Choosing Bending

Players choose Benders at the spawn selection area at the beginning of the season. The element determines the corresponding Bender nation. Bending is intended to remain permanent for the season; changing element is unlikely except possibly in exceptional circumstances.

Bender Nations

The four nations are sub-factions within the Benders faction. They are expected to have their own territories/bases and may have their own leadership and events.

Combat

Bending can be used in PvP. Benders may fight other Benders during training, and faction combat can occur during wars. Bending can affect players and may interact with the environment. Exact energy, cooldown and protection rules are still being finalised.

Selection Area

Bending selection happens during the seasonal selection process. The selection area is only available at the start of a season.
