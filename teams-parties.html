TEAMS & PARTIES.

Beyond SMP uses two different systems for different purposes: Team (ST) and Party (OCAP).

Team (ST)

Team (ST) is the actual social/player team system. It is intended to provide team identity, names, colours and future TAB/chat features.

  • Anyone can create a Team (ST).
  • Teams (ST) can include players from different factions.
  • Opposing factions can be in the same Team (ST).
  • Different Paradis military branches can also be in the same Team (ST).
  • The planned maximum is around 64 players, subject to change.
  • The creator becomes the owner.
  • The owner can manage membership, and members can leave.
  • Team (ST) names and colours can be changed.
  • Additional features may be added later.

Internal ownership/roles may exist, but official owner-style labels are not intended to appear in TAB/chat in a way that could make players think a Team (ST) owner is a server owner.

Party (OCAP)

Party (OCAP) is primarily for shared claims involving multiple people.

  • Anyone can create a Party (OCAP).
  • Players from different factions can be in a Party (OCAP).
  • A Party (OCAP) can be used to manage a shared claim.
  • The creator is the owner, with ownership transfer still being a possible feature.
  • The owner can invite/remove members and members can leave.
  • Everyone in a Party (OCAP) is intended to receive the relevant shared-claim access.

The Difference

Team (ST) = social/team identity.
Names, colours and future TAB features.

Party (OCAP) = shared claims.
Used primarily to give multiple players access to shared property.
