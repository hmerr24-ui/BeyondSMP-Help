TEAMS & PARTIES.

SimpleTeams

Beyond SMP uses SimpleTeams for teams. The currently remembered commands are:

  • /st create [name] [colour]
  • /st invite [name]
  • /st accept
  • /st leave

Some command names/options may change before launch.

Open Claims & Parties

Open Claims & Parties is used for shared claims. Parties are mainly relevant to claims rather than being the main team system.

The exact party commands have not been finalised, so they are intentionally not listed as confirmed commands yet.
